The Story

A passion, plated.

I'm Shabba — a lifestyle chef devoted to bringing people together through high-quality Caribbean cuisine, refined presentation, and service that lingers in the memory. What began as a love of cooking has grown into a luxury dining brand built around the flavours, energy, and heritage of the islands.

From precisely seasoned signatures to the comforting classics, every plate is made with care, creativity, and a respect for detail — drawn from recipes shaped by the vibrant culture of the Caribbean.

Private events, celebrations, corporate gatherings, or quiet special occasions — the intention is always the same: delicious food, premium presentation, and a warm atmosphere that leaves a lasting impression.

Food is more than a meal. It's culture, energy, tradition & luxury — served on one plate.

Good to Know

Questions & Answers

Where do you cater?

We're based in Gloucester and available for private events right across the UK. Wherever your occasion is, get in touch and we'll talk through the details.

Is the food Halal?

Yes — without exception. Every dish is prepared to Halal standards, and we hold a 5★ food hygiene rating from our local authority.

How many guests can you cater for?

From intimate private dinners to larger celebrations and corporate gatherings. Share your numbers when you enquire and we'll tailor the experience to suit.

Can the menu be customised?

Absolutely. The menu is a starting point — flavours and dishes can vary to suit your event and tastes. If you have dietary requirements, mention them in your enquiry and we'll happily accommodate.

How far in advance should I book?

The earlier the better, especially for weekends and peak dates. Send your preferred date through the enquiry form and we'll confirm availability as quickly as we can.

What's included in the experience?

Freshly prepared Caribbean cuisine with premium presentation, served with the care and warmth that defines every Cooking with Shabba event. Tell us your vision and we'll build the rest around it.
