Privacy Policy

This privacy policy explains how Cooking with Shabba ("we", "us") handles any personal information you share with us through this website. This is a placeholder policy and will be reviewed before go-live.

What we collect

When you submit our enquiry form, we collect the information you provide: your name, email address, event type, and optionally your event date, guest count, and message. We do not collect a phone number.

Your information is used solely to respond to your enquiry and discuss your event. We do not sell or share it with third parties for marketing.

How it is processed

Enquiry submissions are processed through Netlify Forms, our hosting provider. No third-party CRM, advertising pixels, or analytics tools are used on this site.

Cookies

This site does not use cookies. We set no tracking, analytics, or advertising cookies, and we do not store anything in your browser.
